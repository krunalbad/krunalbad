## 👨‍💻 About

I’m **Krunal Badami**, a senior **React Native**, **Roku**, and **TV app** engineer from Surat, India.

I work on:

- **React Native** apps for Android & iOS  
- **Roku channel development** (BrightScript + SceneGraph)  
- **Android TV & Apple TV** experiences  
- **OTT & streaming products** with HLS/DASH and DRM  
- High-performance **UI/UX for mobile and big screens**  

I focus on shipping **production-ready streaming apps**, tuning **performance and memory**, and ensuring **smooth D-pad navigation** and **stable playback** across devices.

> SEO Keywords: React Native developer India, Roku developer, BrightScript engineer, TV app developer, OTT app specialist, streaming app engineer.

## 📺 TV & OTT App Expertise

I focus heavily on **TV apps and streaming platforms**:

- End-to-end **Roku channel** development (BrightScript + SceneGraph)  
- **Android TV & Apple TV** navigation, focus handling, and remote UX  
- Custom **video players** with HLS/DASH, DRM, trick-play, and robust error handling  
- **OTT subscription flows**, authentication, entitlement, and deep linking  
- Performance tuning for **low-latency, crash-free TV apps** on constrained hardware  

If you’re building or scaling a **React Native + Roku + TV app** stack, that’s exactly where I work.
